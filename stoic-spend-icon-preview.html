Reads at every size because it's three shapes: a ring, a tick, a dot. Nothing thinner than 58px at the 1024 master, so nothing disappears at 40pt. Corners shown rounded here for preview only — export the full square; iOS applies its own mask.

Launch Screen — mockup

No text, no spinner, no tagline — the mark alone, sized so the transition into Onboarding's loading ring (same mark, same position) reads as one continuous moment rather than two screens.
